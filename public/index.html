ARCHITECTURE

How AquaRoute Conserves Datacenter Water

Datacenters evaporate gallons of water per hour to cool AI hardware clusters. AquaRoute uses semantic caching (0.0 mL water) and routes simple prompts to lightweight models (~0.3 mL) while reserving heavy large models (~5.0 mL) only for complex multi-step reasoning.
